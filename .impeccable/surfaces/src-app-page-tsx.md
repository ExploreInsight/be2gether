# Surface brief: be2gether app shell (feed, search, saved, profile, settings, admin)

Mode: Operate. Audience: small community of friends posting daily, phone-first. Task: read the day, post, reply, find, save.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The feed is a cassette tracklist: numbered posts, ages written as durations (0:47), Side A friends and Side B everyone. It refuses the card-per-post social feed with avatar-left, action-icon row.
OWN-WORLD: Mixtape J-card inlay printed offset: ultramarine spine as the shell (rail and mobile bar), cool off-white card ground, near-black ink, signal orange only for fills and marks (likes, unread), hairline track rules. Condensed heavy display (Big Shoulders Display) for side titles and track numbers; one humanist grotesque (Hanken Grotesk) for text. Dark mode is the same card printed on black stock with a lighter spine blue. Selectable colour themes swap spine and signal hues only.
STORY: A friend opens it, sees tonight's tracks in order, writes a track in one thumb-reach tap, replies, saves, and leaves. Nothing claims a count of friends or popularity.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Desktop: 260px ultramarine rail (wordmark, Feed, Search, Saved, Alerts, Settings), centre 660px column with Side A / Side B titles at 40px+ condensed, composer ("Record a track") at the top, numbered track rows below with durations right-aligned; right column suggestions and trending tags. Phone: spine-blue top bar with wordmark, bottom tab bar (Feed, Search, Record, Alerts, Me) in thumb reach, same track rows.
FORM: Mixtape J-card, assigned, candidate 6 of 7; seed:73ada55712d910f4. Raises: single time axis (from Labanotation), one layer of rank (from Acetate manual).
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

## Signature move
Track rows: the number is the post's position in the day; durations tick as ages; an unread alert is a filled orange tape mark. Reduced-motion or Animations off: no movement at all.

## Unresolved
Photos stay sparse: only user-uploaded post images; seed demo content ships no photographs.
